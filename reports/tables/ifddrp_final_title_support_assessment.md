# IFDDRP Final Title-Support Assessment

Fixed title: **Interpretable Transformer Models for Financial Time Series Forecasting: Discovering Emergent Market Dynamics**

| title_component | support | evidence | limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interpretable | partial | bounded occlusion, lag analysis, probes, identity ablation and sanity controls | one randomisation control failed; sensitivity is not causality |
| Transformer models | strong | real trained 272,449-parameter encoder reconstructed exactly across three seeds | Transformer is not superior to static priors |
| Financial time-series forecasting | strong | 80-instrument daily panel, leakage-corrected chronological design and future stress/downside outcomes | historical test is adaptive and target comparability is imperfect |
| Discovering emergent market dynamics | adversarial/partial | the study discovered that apparent pooled dynamics can emerge from static cross-sectional priors | no useful predictive emergent temporal dynamics were established |

The defensible reading is interpretation 3: apparent dynamics mainly reflected static shortcut structure, with partial support for interpretation 4. The title should be explained as a research question tested adversarially, not as a presupposed positive finding.
